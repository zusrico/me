02 / architecture

commerce
under control.

Shopify remains the source of truth for products, prices, images, variants, and checkout. Next.js App Router structures the experience around that data instead of duplicating it.

03 / decisions

ready when
the store is.

The app can switch between a full shop and a launch landing through one environment variable. Cart operations stay behind an internal API, the Shopify token remains server-side, and cookie preferences are explicit.
