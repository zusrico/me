Jartible already had a strong point of view: expressive products, an irreverent tone, and a distinctly Andalusian identity. The implementation work had to support that character without getting in its way.

02 / implementation

make it
work.

The work focused on bringing the existing design into a reliable WordPress and Elementor experience, keeping collections, product pages, cart interactions, newsletter content, and the brand sections usable across the site.

03 / maintenance

fix fast,
stay sharp.

Beyond the initial implementation, I handled bug fixing and ongoing maintenance: investigating regressions, adjusting HTML and CSS, and keeping the site stable as products, content, and e-commerce needs changed.

the outcome

A working storefront for exploring categories such as camisetas, sudaderas, Club, and Caniculture, with product detail, cart, contact, newsletter, and purchase-support content.

The result preserves the original visual direction while making the underlying WordPress site easier to operate, repair, and evolve.
