01 / product

two sides,
one flow.

FoodLink explores the service from both directions: customers can log in, register, find restaurants, order, follow delivery, revisit favourites, and see their history; administrators can oversee the platform from a separate dashboard.

02 / interface

turn data
into action.

The prototype gives each role a focused workspace. Users get active orders, progress states, promotions, and repeat actions. Admins get activity summaries, quick access, product management, order tables, filters, status changes, and export actions.

03 / prototype

honest about
the next step.

The repository is intentionally a front-end project: flows are represented with hardcoded data, alerts, modals, and client-side interactions. It makes the product direction testable before connecting a real API, database, payments, or delivery service.

the outcome

A concrete starting point for a food delivery product, with the main screens and roles mapped before backend implementation.

The result turns a broad idea into a navigable interface system: login, registration, customer dashboard, administration, products, and orders.
