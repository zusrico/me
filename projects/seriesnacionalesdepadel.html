01 / experience

one sport,
many paths.

The site has to speak to different audiences without splitting the experience: players looking for a team, clubs opening their courts, companies joining the circuit, and organisers communicating each edition.

02 / build

content
in motion.

WordPress and Divi make the public site manageable as the competition changes. Divi Builder keeps the visual system flexible, while custom HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP handle the details beyond the page builder.

03 / data

many countries,
one view.

International database management is part of the work. DBeaver provides a practical workspace to inspect, query, and maintain data across editions and countries, keeping the operational layer reliable behind the public experience.

the format

Amateur teams compete within their level, moving through regular league phases and zonal playoffs before the international master.

The platform supports a circuit that reaches Spain, Portugal, Italy, Sweden, and Mexico, with information for clubs, teams, companies, and players.
