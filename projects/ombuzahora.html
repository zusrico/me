01 / purpose

make room
for the stay.

The site puts the real experience first: apartments for two, three, or four people, private terraces, a shared pool, gardens, parking, and WiFi. The information is there to help someone imagine the stay before making contact.

02 / structure

close to the
right place.

Accommodation, surroundings, gallery, seasonal conditions, and contact are organised as a direct route. Zahora, the beach, Los Caños de Meca, and the Trafalgar lighthouse become part of the decision, not background decoration.

03 / delivery

from idea
to live.

I was responsible for the hosting, design, development, and production launch. WordPress and Divi keep the accommodation content manageable, while custom HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP support the details the project needed.

the outcome

A clear digital front door for Apartamentos Ombú, focused on trust, place, and the practical information guests need before asking about availability.

The result connects the visual identity of the accommodation with a straightforward contact path for reservations in Zahora, Cádiz.
